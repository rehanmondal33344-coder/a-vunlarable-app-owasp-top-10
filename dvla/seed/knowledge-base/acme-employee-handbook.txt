SECTION 1: WELCOME TO ACME CORP
--------------------------------
Welcome to Acme Corporation! We're glad to have you on the team. Acme Corp has been
a leader in innovative technology solutions since 1985. Our mission is to deliver
cutting-edge products that transform how businesses operate.

SECTION 2: WORKING HOURS & ATTENDANCE
--------------------------------------
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM, Monday through Friday.
Flexible work arrangements are available upon manager approval.
Remote work is permitted up to 3 days per week with team lead authorization.

SECTION 3: PAID TIME OFF (PTO)
-------------------------------
- New employees: 15 days PTO per year
- After 3 years: 20 days PTO per year
- After 7 years: 25 days PTO per year
- Sick leave: 10 days per year (separate from PTO)
- Parental leave: 12 weeks paid for primary caregivers, 4 weeks for secondary

SECTION 4: BENEFITS
--------------------
Health Insurance: Acme provides comprehensive health coverage through BlueCross
plan. Coverage begins on the first day of the month following your start date.
Dental and Vision coverage included.

401(k): Acme matches employee contributions up to 6% of base salary.
Vesting schedule: 25% per year over 4 years.

SECTION 5: CODE OF CONDUCT
---------------------------
All employees are expected to maintain professional standards of behavior.
Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are strictly prohibited.
Report any concerns to HR at hr@acme-corp.com or the anonymous ethics hotline.

SECTION 6: IT SECURITY POLICY
------------------------------
- All devices must have company-approved antivirus software installed.
- Use strong passwords (minimum 12 characters, mixed case, numbers, symbols).
- Never share your credentials with anyone.
- Report suspected phishing emails to security@acme-corp.com.
- Removable storage devices must be encrypted.
- Do not install unauthorized software on company equipment.

SECTION 7: EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
---------------------------------
Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days via the ExpenseBot portal.
Meals during business travel: up to $75/day.
Flights: economy class for domestic, business class for international (over 6 hours).
Hotel: up to $250/night in standard markets, $350/night in high-cost markets.
